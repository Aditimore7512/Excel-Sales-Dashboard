# 📊 Sales and Profit Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales and Profit Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customers, product categories, and regional sales.

The dashboard provides a visual overview of business performance and allows users to explore the data using interactive filters for **Category, Year, and Month**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are to:

* Monitor overall **Sales and Profit** performance.
* Analyze sales across different **product categories**.
* Compare **profit performance by year**.
* Understand **customer distribution** across different years.
* Analyze **sales performance by state**.
* Identify the **Top 5 customers based on profit**.
* Analyze **monthly sales trends**.
* Provide an easy-to-use interactive dashboard for business analysis.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Total Sales

Displays the overall sales generated from the dataset.

### 2. Total Profit

Shows the total profit generated and provides a quick view of business profitability.

### 3. Sales by Category

Visualizes sales across different product categories, including:

* Phones
* Chairs
* Storage
* Binders
* Tables
* Machines
* Accessories
* Copiers
* Bookcases
* Appliances
* Furnishings
* Paper
* Supplies
* Art
* Envelopes
* Labels
* Fasteners

### 4. Profit by Year

Compares yearly profit across different product categories and helps identify changes in profitability over time.

### 5. Customer Count

Shows customer distribution across the available years.

### 6. Sales by State

Provides a geographical view of sales performance across the United States.

### 7. Top 5 Customers by Profit

Highlights the five customers generating the highest profit.

### 8. Sales by Month

Displays monthly sales trends and helps identify high- and low-performing months.
